Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Jade Properties Group LLC ("Jade Properties," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects the information you provide when you contact us or submit a property inquiry through our website.

Information we collect

When you fill out a form on our website, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, property address, details about your property, and any other information you choose to provide. We may also receive your information from third-party lead sources such as PropertyLeads.com when you have requested to be contacted.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to evaluate your property, prepare and present cash offers, respond to your inquiry, and communicate with you about selling your property or related services you have expressed interest in. This communication may include phone calls and text messages.

SMS / text messaging

If you opt in to receive text messages, message and data rates may apply and message frequency varies. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. We do not share or sell your mobile opt-in information or consent to any third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. For full details, see our SMS Terms & Conditions.

How we share your information

We may share your information with service providers who help us operate our business (for example, communication or customer-management tools), and with prospective buyers or partners as needed to complete a property transaction. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share text-messaging consent with third parties for marketing purposes. We may disclose information when required by law.

Data retention and security

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and for legitimate business or legal purposes. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may opt out of communications at any time by replying STOP to a text message or by contacting us using the details below.
